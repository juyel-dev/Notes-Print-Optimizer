# What's New

A quick look at the latest improvements.

## Smart PDF Rearrangement

- **Automatic series detection.** Related files such as *Basic Maths and Calculus 1..13 Class Notes* are recognised and ordered naturally.
- **Natural sorting.** Numbers, zero-padded values, ordinals (*2nd, 3rd, 21st*) and Roman numerals (*I, II, XIV*) all sort correctly.
- **One-click Smart Arrange.** A badge shows how many series were found; tap to arrange.
- **Drag & drop.** Reorder files by hand any time - arrow buttons remain as a touch/keyboard fallback.

## Settings & Information Center

- The menu is now a clean **Settings & Information Center**.
- Tools, Privacy, Community, Resources, Legal and Developer sections in one place.
- Read About, User Guide, FAQ, Changelog, Privacy Policy, Terms and the JSL License in-app.
- **Clear Cache** privacy action, feedback form, Telegram community links and contact details.
