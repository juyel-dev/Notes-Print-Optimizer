# About Notes Print Optimizer

**Notes Print Optimizer** is a mobile-first, privacy-first print optimizer and PDF engine for Physics Wallah (PW) and lecture class notes. It converts dark-background lecture slides into clean, print-ready PDFs that save ink and paper.

## What it does

- **Strips dark backgrounds** from lecture slides so they print cleanly on white paper.
- **Sharpens ink strokes** and text for legibility at small sizes.
- **Rearranges pages into N-up grids** (1-up to 10-up) to fit many slides on a single sheet.
- **Detects related PDF series** (for example *Calculus 1..13 Class Notes*) and orders them naturally.
- **Reports real savings** - estimated ink and paper reduction for every job.

## Privacy by design

Everything runs **100% on your device**. Your PDFs are processed in your browser and are never uploaded to any server. No account is required and no files are collected.

## Technology

- Next.js + React + TypeScript
- PDF.js and pdf-lib for rendering and composition
- Rust-compiled WebAssembly kernels for fast image processing
- Installable Progressive Web App with offline support
