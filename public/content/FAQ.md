# Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this app upload my files?**
No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your PDFs never leave your device.

**Do I need to create an account?**
No. The app is free to use with no sign-up.

**Which file types are supported?**
PDF files. You can upload one or many and they will be merged.

**Why do my slides look washed out after processing?**
The optimizer removes dark backgrounds to save ink. Try increasing **Contrast** or **Sharpen** in the settings, then re-process.

**How much ink and paper can I save?**
Typically 70-85% ink and up to 75% paper when using a 4-Up grid, depending on your slides.

**Can I reorder or remove pages?**
Yes. Exclude pages in the Optimize step, and reorder or remove whole files in the Upload step.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. Once loaded, the app works offline as a Progressive Web App.

**What is Smart Arrange?**
It automatically detects related PDF series (for example *Calculus 1..13*) and orders them naturally, so you don't have to sort them by hand.

Use **Send Feedback** in the menu and choose the right category.
